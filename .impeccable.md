# Impeccable — project design context

## Design Context

### Users

BwanaBet betting customers in Zambia. They get one free spin per wheel-day
(resets 06:00 CAT), for cash prizes of K10–K200 credited to their betting
account. Roughly 5,000–6,000 people spin each day.

They reach it two ways, both first-class:

- **Phones** — most traffic, narrow viewports, no hover, often a slow mobile
  network. The widget iframe is `display:none` until it loads, so it is already
  deprioritised by the browser.
- **Shared shop PCs** — several customers use one machine in a betting shop,
  logging in and out in sequence. Account switching mid-session is a real,
  handled case, not an edge case.

The job to be done is small and daily: *check if today's spin is still there,
take it, find out if I won.* The whole interaction is under thirty seconds.

### Brand Personality

**Big-win carnival energy.** Loud, celebratory, jackpot-style — a fairground
prize booth, not a bank. Gold, motion, a sense of occasion. Unapologetically
promotional in the invitation.

The one place that energy stops is the outcome. ~99.65% of spins lose, so the
loss screen carries almost all of the emotional weight of this product. Its job
is **"come back tomorrow"** — warm and forward-looking, never rubbing it in,
never begging.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Inherit the house style. Do not invent one.** These are measured off the live
bwanabet.com, not chosen:

| Token | Value | Measured from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page background | `rgb(16,16,16)` near-black | `body` |
| Brand accent | `rgb(255,241,0)` electric lemon | REGISTER button |
| Text on accent | `rgb(0,1,1)` | REGISTER button |
| Type | **Roboto Condensed** 400/700 | site-wide |
| Corner radius | **`0px`** | every site button |
| Button case | UPPERCASE | site-wide |

The site is near-black, electric lemon, hard-cornered and condensed — a
street-poster / arcade language, not a soft dashboard one. Every surface the
wheel adds must speak it.

**Warm gold is reserved for one thing: prize amounts.** The wheel's gold
(`#ffd700`) reads as *money* precisely because the rest of the site is lemon.
Keep gold for K-amounts only; use brand lemon for actions and emphasis.

**The chatbot popup is the reference for the bubble.** The site's AI BOT speaks
in a small dark slate bubble: **sentence case**, plain white ~12px, ~8px radius,
a small circular × at the top-right corner, mascot beside it. It is understated
on purpose — the character carries the personality, not the typography. The
wheel's bubble matches that pattern, with the gift box as the character.

**Two contexts, two registers — this is the key rule.**

The wheel spans two environments and they must NOT be styled the same:

1. **On bwanabet.com (the bubble, the trigger button).** This is a guest in
   someone else's house. Speak the host's language: near-black, lemon, sharp
   corners, condensed caps, and specifically the chatbot's small dark slate
   bubble pattern. Anything louder reads as a third-party advert.

2. **Inside the widget iframe (every screen).** The widget already has a strong,
   deliberate identity that predates this work — **an arcade prize cabinet**:
   - a rounded dark-navy panel framed by a running border of **gold marquee
     bulbs**, the signature asset
   - a **chrome/silver wheel rim** with its own bulbs and a gold drop pointer
   - **candy-coloured segments** — magenta K50, lemon K200, cyan K10, green K20,
     orange K100, grey TRY AGAIN TOMORROW
   - a **glossy domed SPIN/STOP button** at the hub
   - gold headline type over it all

   Every result card — win, loss, done — renders **inside** that cabinet, on top
   of the wheel, with the bulbs and header still visible around it. So a result
   card cannot introduce a foreign visual language; it will be seen in the same
   glance as the bulbs and chrome.

**Design INTO the cabinet, never over it.** The marquee bulbs are the most
distinctive thing this product owns. New elements should extend that vocabulary
(bulb strips, chrome bezels, domed buttons), not replace it with hard-cut
poster geometry.

**Anti-references:** rounded navy cards with soft gold gradients and no cabinet
context (the generic AI result); flat black/lemon poster geometry *inside* the
cabinet, which clashes with the bulbs and chrome; casino neon; purple/cyan;
anything that would look at home in a SaaS dashboard.

**Existing debt to fix, not preserve:** the current screens use gradient-filled
text (`background-clip: text`) for every heading. It is a recognisable AI tell,
it renders unevenly on low-DPI shop monitors, and it fails outright in a few
older mobile browsers, leaving invisible text. Replace with solid gold.

### Design Principles

1. **Celebrate the invitation, tell the truth about the outcome.** Carnival
   energy belongs before the spin. After it, honesty — a customer who is
   congratulated and then shown "better luck next time" learns not to trust the
   next message.
2. **Gold is money.** Reserve it for prizes and the single primary action.
   Gold on a losing screen is a lie told in colour.
3. **The loss screen is the product.** 996 in 1,000 customers only ever see
   that screen. It deserves more design attention than the win screen, not less.
4. **Works at 320px and on a 1440px shop monitor.** No hover-only affordances.
5. **Motion carries excitement, gradients don't.** Staggered entrances and
   weight contrast, never decorative gradient fills.
6. **Never block the host page.** `embed.js` runs on BwanaBet's own site,
   dependency-free, and must fail silently rather than break their page.
